Official Kitchen Guidelines & Safety Protocol

KITCHEN GUIDELINES

BY: ATE TINA

  • 🐾 Pets are not allowed in the kitchen.
  • 🥩 Please count meat according to the number of students and place in clean tupperware (70 pcs per tupperware).
  • 🔒 Please close and lock the door when kitchen is not in use.
  • 🍴 Sterilize the spoons and forks after every meal.
  • 🔪 Sterilize all hanging utensils (e.g., ladle, tong, knives, etc.).
  • 🧼 Make sure to wash curtains twice a month.
  • 🧊 Make sure to turn off the refrigerator before cleaning and handle the fridge with care and not roughly.
  • 🧽 Clean the refrigerator every week and use cloth or face towel to clean the refrigerator.
  • ⚠️ Don't use sharp objects such as knives, ice picks, etc., on the fridge.
  • ❄️ Set the right temperature for the fridge (1-6°C) and for the freezer (-15°C).
  • 🚪 Minimize the number of times you open the door in the refrigerator.
  • 🥬 Observe proper segregation of frozen foods and vegetables.
  • 🔥 Clean the burner or gas stoves twice (2) a day after cooking.
  • 🗑️ Make sure all trashcans are empty and clean every night.
  • 🫑 Make sure to remove the plastic cellophane before placing the vegetables to the designated area.
  • 🧑‍🍳 Ensure that one person is in the kitchen watching the food being cooked and watching the stove in case of fire or emergency.
  • 🥣 All kitchen materials taken from the kitchen must be returned and washed immediately.
  • ✨ Maintain cleanliness and orderliness of the kitchen.
Prepared by: IRISH MAY BULAN (Education Manager) Approved by: MANLUNAS, CRISTINA S. (Cook)

ADDITIONAL KITCHEN GUIDELINES

Passerelles Numériques Safe & Respectful Environment Rules

1. No Cooking Beyond Kitchen Hours

Only the kitchen team may cook during designated hours to conserve gas and electricity. Cooking after dinner and in between classes is strictly prohibited.

2. Kitchen Locked at 9:00 PM

The kitchen will be locked after dinner and cleaning at 9:00 PM to ensure no one uses kitchen tools or equipment beyond operational hours.

3. Kettles and Water Availability

Kettles, pitcher, and gallon of water are available in the kitchen. Kettles must stay in the kitchen. A gallon of water will be placed outside once closed.

4. All Sponsored Food Intended for Everyone

Cooking or preparing sponsored food is only permitted when shared with all students. Always ask for proper permission before serving.

Simple PN Kitchen Workflow

1. Suki Delivery Arrives (+ Stock In): 1st Year or 2nd Year Coordinator logs the supplier receipt under Deliveries. Stock automatically increases.

2. Daily Cooking (- Stock Out): Kitchen team logs ingredients taken out under Book Out Usage. Stock automatically decreases.

3. Weekly Budget: Planned Weekly Budget − Weekly Delivery Receipts = Remaining Cash Budget.

4. Weekly Report: Ate Tina opens Weekly Reports and clicks Print / Save Weekly Report for PN staff—no manual rewriting!
